# Kittiwake Mobile sample pack

Kittiwake Mobile is an invented company. Every town, plan, handset, name, order
number and message in these files was generated by make_kittiwake.py with seed
20260925. None of it is real, and none of it came from a real customer.

The SMS Spam Collection beside it (sms_spam/) is the one exception: a public
research corpus by Tiago A. Almeida and Jose Maria Gomez Hidalgo, published by the
UCI Machine Learning Repository under CC BY 4.0,
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/228/sms+spam+collection

Real public datasets, each already in this image, so nothing downloads in a lab:

- sentiment_sentences/: UCI Sentiment Labelled Sentences, Kotzias et al. (2015),
  CC BY 4.0, https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/331/sentiment+labelled+sentences
- online_retail/: UCI Online Retail, Daqing Chen (2015), CC BY 4.0,
  https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail (converted to CSV)
- glove/: GloVe 6B 50d, Pennington, Socher and Manning (2014), Public Domain
  Dedication and License 1.0, https://nlp.stanford.edu/projects/glove/
  (the 40,000 most frequent words only)
- nltk_data/corpora/reuters: Reuters-21578 ApteMod as packaged by NLTK;
  collection at https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/137/reuters+21578+text+categorization+collection, CC BY 4.0
- news_headlines/: UCI News Aggregator, Gasparetti (2016), CC BY 4.0,
  https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/359/news+aggregator (15,000 per category)
- eng_spa/: English-Spanish sentence pairs from the Tatoeba Project, CC BY 2.0 FR,
  https://tatoeba.org, as packaged by https://www.manythings.org/anki/ (pairs of up to 8 words)
