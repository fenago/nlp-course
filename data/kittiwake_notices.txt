Planned maintenance: Kittiwake Mobile engineers will upgrade the mast at St. Aldric's Road, Gullhaven, on 14 October from 1 a.m. to approx. 5 a.m. Calls and data may drop during this window. Customers on Unlimited Plus won't be charged for Wi-Fi calling in the meantime. Questions? Text HELP to 2020 or visit kittiwake.example/status.

From 1 November the Flex 30 plan rises from $30.00 to $32.50 a month. Customers who joined before 1 Sept. keep their current price until their contract ends. Dr. Ines Varga, Kittiwake's head of pricing, said the change funds new 5G masts in Saltmoor and Port Ember.

Outage update, 3:40 p.m.: data services in Cragwell and Marrowby are restored. A fibre cut near Eastholm Quay caused the outage. Affected customers will receive a $5.00 credit on their next bill. We're sorry for the disruption.

The Nimbus X2 software update (version 4.2) is paused while we investigate reports that some handsets won't restart. If your Nimbus X2 is stuck, hold the power and volume-down buttons for 20 seconds. Don't return the phone to a shop until you have tried this.

Kittiwake Mobile now supports eSIM on the Corvid 5 and the Aster Fold. Existing customers can switch in the app under Account, SIM. It takes about two minutes and doesn't change your number or your plan.
