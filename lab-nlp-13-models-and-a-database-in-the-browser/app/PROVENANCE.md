# Where notices.json and queries.json come from

Kittiwake Mobile is the course's invented carrier. Nothing in these files is
real: no person, company, place, number or price.

- `n01` to `n05`: the five notices of the course data pack
  (`/opt/nlplab/data/kittiwake_notices.txt`, generated by
  `images/nlplab/generators/make_kittiwake.py`), text verbatim, titles added.
- `n06` to `n48`: invented for this lab on 2026-09-25, in the same style.
- `queries.json`: 16 questions invented for this lab. Their answers are held
  by the checkpoint, not here.
